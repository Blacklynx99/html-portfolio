The Movies I've seen so far

My top 3 favourite Movies

Captain America: Brave New World

Captain America: Brave New World was a very engaging continuation of the Captain America franchise. I like the fact that Sam Wilson did not take the super soldier serum, which makes his fights with super powered individuals more exciting and makes him more relatable. Overall the story was great and is a good way to kickstart the next phase of the Avengers franchise.

Thunderbolts*

Thunderbolts explored the hole that the vetaran Avengers left. The world has no more heroes to defend them, and this pushed clandestine government sectors to make their own ideal hero. The main characters are outcasts and misfits who don't fit the conventional hero role. Some struggle with a lack of purpose but had to rise to the occassion to help a friend in need and do what is right. In the end, these new heroes decide to form a new Avengers team, but come to blows with Captain America, as he also has created a new Avengers team.

The Fantastic Four: First Steps

This movie wasn't too bad. I wasn't really a big fan of the Fantastic Four, but this movie seemed okay, storywise. I didn't like the fact that everything looked like it took place in the 1960s, especially when there seems to be advanced technology involved. It was different, and I like different, but it was weird at the same time. Baby Franklin was weird too. The CGI looked odd made Franklin look like one of those annoying dancing babies in those AI videos on the internet. Besides that, I liked the movie and look forward to what comes next.
